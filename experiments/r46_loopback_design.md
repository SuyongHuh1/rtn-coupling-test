# R46: measurement-chain positive control (USB-6421 AO -> AI loopback). Pre-registered design.

Fixed on 2026-11-24, before any loopback record was analysed. Not to be changed after the results are seen.

## Chain
- **Hardware:**
  - NI USB-6421, AO 0 -> AI 0+ and AO GND -> AI 0- (differential), short wires.
  - AO: 16 bit, ±10 V, LSB = 0.305 mV.
  - AI range: ±0.2 V.
- **Timing:**
  - AO and AI are separate finite tasks, each on its own sample clock at 10 kS/s.
  - There is no shared start trigger. AO is started first, then AI.
  - AI reads 4096 samples. Each AO file has 5096 samples, so the AI window falls inside it.
- **Code:** LabVIEW VI (DAQmx) that plays each `ao_in/*.csv` and writes `ai_out/<same name>.csv`.

## Step 0: noise test (decides only the step amplitude)
- **Files:** `t1_zero`, `t2_sine` (10 mV, 500-sample period), `t3_square_3lsb`.
- **Measured:** chain noise RMS (after linear detrending of `t1_zero`), drift, and gain.
- **Amplitude rule** (fixed now):
  - The two step heights are a1 = n1 LSB and a2 = n2 LSB.
  - n2 is chosen so that a2 / noise_RMS is the integer-LSB value closest to 2.5. This puts it inside the simulation range 1.5–4.
  - n1 = round(1.7 n2), so a1 / a2 ≈ 1.7.
  - If no integer n2 gives a step-to-noise ratio of 1.5–4, the experiment is no-go.

## Records (generated by `r46_loopback_make.py`, seed 4646)
- **Model:** two traps, equation (1) of the paper, φ = 0.5, symmetric J. Exact continuous-time simulation, sampled at the AO rate.
- **Traps:**
  - Mean dwells per state are log-uniform in 10–100 AI samples.
  - Occupancy is 0.3–0.7.
  - Parameters are drawn independently per record.
- **Conditions:**

  | J | Records |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | 40 |
  | +1 k_BT | 30 |
  | +2 k_BT | 30 |
  | −1 k_BT | 30 |

  The J = −1 condition is dropped first if time runs out.
- **Playback order:** random. File names are `NNN_<hash>.csv`; the mapping to J is kept in `r46_truth.json` and is not used by the analysis.

## Analysis (no change to the pipeline or its hyperparameters)
- **Test:** the paper's two-trap calibrated test (`r8_calib.job`, RTN_MS=3, RTN_RESTART=3, RTN_JOINT=0), B = 99, p ≤ 0.05. Checklist steps 1–4.
- **Reported:**
  - FPR at J = 0 (Wilson CI).
  - Detection rate per J (Wilson CI).
  - Median of Ĵ against the true J.
  - Coverage of the bootstrap interval.
  - The four published rules on the same records, reported like the "real-noise control" column of Table I.
- **Reporting:** all results are reported unchanged, whatever they are.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-24, before any coupled record was played)
- **Reason:**
  - At the pre-registered AI range (±0.2 V), the step-0 noise test (`results/r46/step0_noise.json`) gives a chain noise of 32.6 µV RMS.
  - The smallest AO step (1 LSB = 0.305 mV) is therefore 9.4 times the noise, above the pre-registered step-to-noise range of 1.5–4.
- **Change:**
  - AO 0 -> resistive divider (about 1/10: R1 ≈ 9.1 kΩ from AO 0 to the node, R2 ≈ 1 kΩ from the node to AO GND) -> AI 0+.
  - AI 0- is wired to the bottom of the divider (AO GND), so the input does not float.
  - The AI range stays at ±0.2 V.
- **Timing:** this amendment was recorded before any coupled record was generated or played.
- **Re-measured with the divider in place, before the main measurement:**
  1. **Divider ratio:** measured from known DC levels (AO 0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 1.6 V). The ratio is the slope of a linear fit, and the measured value replaces the nominal one.
  2. **Noise:** RMS and lag-1 autocorrelation of a zero record, after linear detrending. If the RMS exceeds 2 × 32.6 µV or |lag-1| > 0.2, the result is reported and the experiment stops.
- **Amplitudes:** these replace the "Amplitude rule" of step 0. They are fixed from the re-measured ratio and noise and written below before playback starts. They are not changed after results are seen.
  - **Smallest step:** a_small = n LSB × ratio, with n the integer that puts a_small / noise_RMS closest to 2.5. It must lie within 2–3, otherwise the result is reported and the experiment stops.
  - **Other trap:** its step is a_small / r, with r drawn per record uniformly in [0.15, 1] (smallest-to-largest step ratio as in the simulator) and rounded to whole AO LSB.
  - **Which trap is smaller:** chosen at random per record.
- **Unchanged:** conditions, counts, seed, B = 99, checklist and reported metrics.

## Amendment 1a (2026-11-24, still before any coupled record)
- **Reason:** the available resistors are 10 kΩ, 220 Ω and 100 Ω.
- **Change:** R1 = 10 kΩ and R2 = 220 Ω, a nominal ratio of 0.0215 (about 1/46) instead of about 1/10.
  - One AO LSB then gives about 6.6 µV at AI 0.
  - With the step-0 noise, the smallest step needs about 12–13 LSB.
- **Unchanged:** everything else in Amendment 1, including the measured ratio replacing the nominal one, the noise stop rule and the amplitude rule.

## Step 1 result and fixed amplitudes (2026-11-24, before any coupled record)
- **Re-measurement with the divider:** see `results/r46/step1_divider.json`.
  - Measured ratio: 0.021423 (nominal 0.0215). The DC fit residuals are within 3.4 µV.
  - Noise: 24.6 and 25.3 µV RMS, lag-1 −0.01 and −0.07, so the stop rule is not triggered.
- **Fixed amplitudes:**
  - One AO LSB gives 6.538 µV at AI.
  - Smallest step: n = 10 LSB = 65.4 µV, a step-to-noise of 2.62.
  - Other trap: round(10 / r) LSB, with r ~ U[0.15, 1] per record. This gives 10–67 LSB, i.e. 65–438 µV.
  - Which trap carries the smaller step is random per record.
  - The baseline is AO = 0 V.

## Execution notes and deviations (recorded after the analysis; no result-dependent change)
- **Checklist step 4 (drive model) was not run.** The loopback records contain no common drive, and step 4 only removes calls. The figures after steps 1–2 are therefore an upper bound for the whole procedure, as in Table II of the paper.
- **Two code fixes in `r46_loopback_analyze.py` during execution.** Neither changes the pipeline, its hyperparameters or the decision rule.
  1. Importing `r24_jci` parsed the command line and crashed at the first coupled call (commit 13c100e). The first two laptop runs stopped before saving any result. The second run used a stale copy of the script.
  2. `summary()` read the results file one level too shallow. It was fixed, and the original file was restored from the laptop copy.
- **Additional QC (not pre-registered):** each AI record was aligned to its AO waveform.
  - Delay: 135–146 samples.
  - Gain: 0.0209–0.0221.
  - Residual RMS: 20.9–25.4 µV.
  - No record has a robust z above 5 on any of these measures.
  - Heavy-tailed noise (excess kurtosis 1–5) was already present in the divider zero records before playback.
- **Results not quoted in the paper** (see `results/r46/r46_results.json`, `summary`):
  - At J = +2, 21/30 records remain called after steps 1–2.
  - Bootstrap interval coverage among called records: 25/27, 24/29 and 26/29 (75/85 = 0.88).
  - Ĵ medians: 0.94, 1.77 and −0.87.
